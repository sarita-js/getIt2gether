Why GetIt2gether?

Built for real life,
not a productivity podcast

No hustle-culture guilt trips. Just your tasks, your vibe, and actually useful reminders.

👤
Your Account, Your Data
Tasks are saved to your account and persist across sessions. Log in from any device and your tasks are right where you left them.
🔔
Meme Reminders
6 notification packs — Chaotic Energy, Hype Beast, Sigma Grindset, Cottagecore and more. Get roasted, hyped, or gently reminded, your choice.
📲
Background Notifications
Real OS push notifications that fire even when the app isn't open. Deadlines can't sneak up on you when your phone is literally telling you about them.
🎨
7 Personality Themes
Night Owl, Zen Mode, Grind Set, Bubbly, Terminal, Cottagecore, and Cyberpunk. The whole app — fonts, colors, copy, category names — transforms to match your vibe.
📂
5 Life Categories
School, Work, Appointments, Chores, and General To-Do. Category names even change with your theme — your "Homework" becomes "The Missions" in Grind mode.

Smart Urgency System
Tasks automatically color-code by how soon they're due. Overdue tasks surface immediately. The dashboard tells you exactly where to focus first.
